Workplace

  • Twenty years ago, FPT nurtured the aspiration to build a distinctive university environment where students learn to work, to live, and to lead the future. From that aspiration, FPT University (FPTU) was born with the spirit of "real learning - real work - real success," pioneering the integration of businesses and practical projects into the classroom, opening a path for Vietnamese students to reach out to the world. Over nearly two decades, FPTU has continuously transformed itself in line with the changing world, from a technology university to a global technology, entrepreneurship, and business university . Technology is the foundation for innovation, entrepreneurship is the mindset of self-reliance, and the global environment is an integrated learning space – all guided by Vietnamese identity: flexibility, resilience, and responsibility. Each FPTU student, upon entering the world, not only carries professional skills and a global mindset but also embodies Vietnamese resilience, ready to shape the future for themselves, their community, and their country.

  • Student life at FPT University is a dynamic learning journey, seamlessly blending knowledge, practical experience, and a multicultural environment. Students have the opportunity to study, interact, and gain experience across five campuses nationwide, while also participating in a diverse system of clubs, extracurricular activities, community involvement, and cultural and sports events. Furthermore, international exchange programs and overseas semesters help students broaden their global perspective, enhance their integration skills, and enrich their comprehensive academic experiences.
